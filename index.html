Welcome to Soléa Bay

A hush of turquoise, a world away

Soléa Bay is a collection of thirty-two villas set along a protected lagoon. Mornings begin with coral-pink light and a swim before breakfast; evenings end on the deck, lanterns lit, the water turning gold.

Everything here is designed to feel unhurried — from private plunge pools and open-air bathrooms to a spa built over the water and kitchens that cook what the lagoon and nearby farms provide.
